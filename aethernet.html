▸ WHAT THIS INSTRUMENT IS

The Aethernet is a four-station broadcast console for the Corridor Templars lore corpus. It does work that has, until now, been done by hand: generating new documents in the corpus voice, transmuting research dossiers into lore and back again, filing the small anomalies that arrive in stranger's mouths, and triaging fringe claims into rival readings. The console runs in your browser. It calls the Anthropic API with the key you provide. Nothing routes through a server you do not own.

▸ STATIONS

NIGHT FORGE · runs while you sleep. Feed it the master corpus, set the count and direction, press BEGIN BROADCAST. By morning the archive will hold new field reports, dossiers, handler logs, and lore fragments in the established voice. Each document is saved as it lands so the run survives reload, network blips, and rate limits. Two modes: master extend grows what exists; spin-off generates new works in the universe.

TRANSMUTER · feed in any single document; the transmuter returns it as its other side. Research dossier becomes in-world lore. Lore fragment becomes a Layer-A research dossier with confidence matrix. Or generate a spin-off document orbiting the source. The seam stays visible.

THE LEDGER · the Tuesday Ledger. A private Echo Speech log structured as a classified instrument. File the codewords overheard, the songs at the wrong volume, the phrases that arrived through three channels in two days. Each entry tagged with date, source, context, and the small private moment that may have preceded the window. Across enough entries the pattern, if there is one, shows itself. All data stays on this device unless you click ANALYZE.

RIVAL READINGS · paste any claim. The instrument returns five rigorous competing interpretations, each rated CONFIRMED / OPEN / PARTIAL / CONTRADICTED / UNFALSIFIABLE with sources cited where the public record supports them. Less for generating full dossiers — more for fast first-pass on anything that crosses your desk before you decide whether it deserves one.

▸ THE SEAM

The corpus distinguishes between Layer A (research dossiers — public-record claims rated against the evidence) and Layer B (fiction — the Corridor Templars universe, Acts 0 through 4). The Aethernet preserves this distinction. Documents generated by the Night Forge carry FICTION or RESEARCH headers as appropriate. The Transmuter's mode setting controls which way the seam is flipped, never blurred. If you find yourself uncertain whether what you are reading is real or fictional, the header always tells you. The corpus does not hide its seams. The seams are the most important part.

▸ RELAY & SIGMA

The console runs on the S5 forge backend with the house key — the same engine the Live Forge and Bookforge use. No API key is stored or transmitted from your browser. Overnight runs are authorized by your S5 session and paid in Sigma, server-side, so the longer you let it broadcast the more Sigma it spends. Estimated progress is displayed live as each station runs. Log in via the S5 console (dossierforge → GO TO S5 LOGIN) to begin.
